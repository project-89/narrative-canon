Alice's Adventure

Alice was walking through the forest when she met Bob. Bob was carrying a mysterious book.

"What's that book?" Alice asked.

"It's a magic spellbook," Bob replied. "It belongs to the wizard who lives in the tower."

Alice and Bob decided to visit the wizard together. They walked to the tall tower at the edge of the forest.

The wizard welcomed them into his tower. His name was Merlin, and he was very old and wise.

"I've been expecting you," Merlin said. "The book has chosen you both for a special quest."

Alice looked at Bob, and they both nodded. Their adventure was just beginning.
