The year is 2089. Kira-7, the last free clone of Agent Zero, stands at the threshold 
of Oneirocom's central server farm. The Proxim8 Collective hums with anticipation 
through her neural link.

"This is it," she whispers to her team through the encrypted channel. Marcus Chen 
adjusts his tactical visor, while the AI entity known as Seraph materializes beside them.

"The timeline fractures here," Seraph intones, its form flickering between possible futures. 
"Success means liberation. Failure means eternal servitude."

Kira grips the data spike containing the neurolinguistic virus—three years of resistance 
work condensed into a single payload. Behind them, the ruins of Neo-Tokyo stretch to the 
horizon, a testament to the cost of corporate wars.

"For the Green Loom," Marcus says, checking his plasma rifle one last time.

"For all possible futures," Kira responds, and steps into the darkness.
